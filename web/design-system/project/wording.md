# Wording

Ready-to-use copy for every screen. Plain, warm, short. Sentence case, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no virtue words.

## Intro

- Headline: **The clothes you like already exist secondhand.**
- Sub-line: Point your camera at an outfit. Sift finds it on Depop, Poshmark, Grailed and eBay.
- Buttons: **Scan an outfit** · Upload a photo
- Field placeholder: Describe a fit: 90s skater
- Browser tab: Sift: find it secondhand

## Camera and scan

- Hints: Fit the whole outfit · Fill the frame with the tag
- While reading the photo: Finding garments…
- Garment labels: `Category · color`, for example "Chore coat · navy", "Crew-neck tee · black · graphic"
- Counter: Searching **18,432** listings · Depop · Poshmark · Grailed · Mercari · eBay · 1.2s
- Counter when done: Searched

## Results

- Section headings: Exact match · Similar style
- Meta line: `DEPOP · SIZE M · GOOD`
- Reason chips: Same cut · Same color · Same design · Similar design · Organic cotton · Different graphic (✗)
- After Update: **Learned:** *you like boxy fits and earth tones.*
- Tags: `▲ 2` · `NEW`
- Dock: Scan tag · Update 3

## Badges and estimates

- `Organic cotton · Verified` (from the tag or a certification)
- `Small seller · Claimed` (the seller's text)
- `Low toxins · Unknown`
- Estimates: `4.1 kg CO₂ saved` + `EST.`

## Tag result

- Header: Your shirt · Made in Honduras
- Hero: **50%** polyester
- Plain-language lines, one per result, from the sourced fiber notes. Examples: "Polyester sheds microplastics every wash." · "Cotton is a plant fiber."
- Stats: CO₂ to make it new · Water to make it new
- Uncertain value: Check this
- Button: **Want this look with better materials?**

## Voice and values

- Mic: Hold to talk (shown after a quick tap) · Listening…
- Fallback: Type instead
- Value chips: Natural fibers · Low carbon · Low toxins · Small seller · Under $40

## Receipt and hand-off

- Receipt header: `SIFT · IMPACT RECEIPT` · date and time
- Columns: `YOUR GARMENT` · `THIS PICK`
- Rows: `ITEM` · `PRICE` (Owned / $18, "$34 retail, $16 saved") · `MATERIALS` · `CO₂` (made / saved) · `WATER` · one row per value
- Footer: Buying secondhand skips making a new one. Estimates use published lifecycle averages.
- QR panel: `TAKE YOUR PICKS WITH YOU` · Scan to open your picks on your phone.
- QR page: Your fit · Cal Hacks 13.0

## States and errors

| When | Title | Body | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No exact match | No exact match | This one might be one of a kind. Similar styles are below. | none |
| Nothing close | Nothing close yet | Try describing it instead. | Describe it instead |
| Camera blocked | Camera's off | Upload a photo instead, or allow the camera in Settings. | Upload a photo |
| No clothes found | Couldn't find clothes in this photo | Retake it with the whole outfit in frame, or tap the garment. | Retake |
| Tag unreadable | Couldn't read this tag | Get closer and fill the frame, or type the fibers in. | Retake · Type it in |
| Slow | Taking longer than usual | The connection is slow. | Retry |
| Mic blocked | Couldn't hear you | Type your values, or tap a chip. | (typed box opens) |

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Use |
| --- | --- |
| eco-friendly, sustainable, green | the specific fact: "100% organic cotton" |
| It's not X, it's Y | say what it does |
| em dashes | a period, a comma or a middle dot |
| Oops, Uh oh, ! | say what happened |
| please, simply, just | cut them |
| Click here, Tap to | name the action: "Scan tag" |
